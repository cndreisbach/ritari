Reviewed the idea of a Pi extension for Ritari. Key finding: Pi already discovers ~/.agents/skills (the default rit skills install target) and loads AGENTS.md, so "use rit" and "read open threads first" need no extension at all.

Agreed to scope any extension to what a skill cannot do: a typed tool surface, deterministic session_start injection, and enforcement of write/move policy.
